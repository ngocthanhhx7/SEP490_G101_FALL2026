# P30 handoff — Customer rating and complaint

## Scope

- WBS-048 / UC-62: Customer rates the assigned Sitter once after a `COMPLETED`
  or contract-approved `STAFF_CLOSED` visit.
- WBS-049 / UC-63: Customer submits one open complaint per visit within seven
  days of the server-derived completion timestamp.
- WBS-050 / UC-64: Staff moves a case through review and resolution; Customer may
  disagree once; Admin makes the final close decision.

## Implementation

Backend service ports are in `backend/src/services/reviews/` and
`backend/src/services/complaints/`. Their routers are export-only modules in
`backend/src/routes/review.routes.js` and `backend/src/routes/complaint.routes.js`.
Frontend adapters and screens live under `frontend/src/services/` and
`frontend/src/pages/customer/` / `frontend/src/pages/staff/`.

Commands require an idempotency key. Complaint mutations require the current
`expectedVersion`; repository implementations must make duplicate-open checks,
state transitions, audit/outbox writes and settlement holds atomic. Evidence is
serialized as private media IDs only; no public image URL is accepted or
generated by this package.
