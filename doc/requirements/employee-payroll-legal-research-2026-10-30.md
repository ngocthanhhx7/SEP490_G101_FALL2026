## Legal Requirements Reflected

- Articles32/90: part-time is employment; equivalent employment rights apply. Contract
  wage and contracted working hours must satisfy the applicable minimum.
- Decree293 Article3: regions I/II/III/IV minimum monthly wages are
  5,310,000/4,730,000/4,140,000/3,700,000 VND; hourly25,500/22,700/20,000/17,800 VND.
  Confirm the actual workplace commune/ward against the annex. Article4 explains
  conversion for weekly/daily/piece work. Do not use a fixed four-week month.
- Articles94/95: pay fully and on time; provide a statement including overtime and deductions.
  Base salary is not contingent on customers paying their bookings.
- Article97: monthly pay once or twice monthly at a recurring agreed date. Hour/day/week
  wages may be aggregated by agreement but not for more than15 days. Calling a weekly
  hourly contract “monthly payment” does not remove this limit; actual contract form matters.
- Article98: ordinary/rest-day/holiday overtime minima150/200/300%, night premium>=30%,
  with additional night-overtime rules. Do not label commission as overtime compensation.
  Actual overtime assessment is required before payroll approval.
- Article101: agreed voluntary advances bear no interest; separate mandatory situations
  include qualifying civic-duty absence and annual leave. The once/month discretionary
  limit must not override statutory entitlements.
- Articles102/127: lawful wage deductions are constrained; disciplinary cash fines or wage
  cuts are prohibited. The30% damage-deduction cap is not a generic limit or permission
  for every payroll adjustment. Advance repayment is recorded separately under agreement.
- Article104: bonus rules must be published and, where applicable, employee representative
  consultation is required. KPI conditions cannot retrospectively change earned salary.
- Employee tax/insurance must use current applicable law and actual employee records.
  The former partner booking-tax split is superseded. Part-time is not automatically exempt.
  No blanket tax or insurance rate is activated by this research.

## Owner-Approved Demo Economics

10m full-time gross monthly base and3m part-time gross monthly base at20h/week were
confirmed. Part-time equivalent is about34,615 VND/hour under52/12 conversion, exceeding
the2026 regionI hourly floor. Actual hours, region, leave, overtime and statutory assessment
still need contract/period records.

5% commission and500k/1m/1.5m (full-time),150k/300k/450k (part-time) monthly KPI bonuses
were proposed as a controllable demo policy, not a legally prescribed or verified market rate.
Owner approved them in this session. A20m service contribution at100% KPI gives12m gross
full-time compensation; company margin must also cover employer insurance, materials,
facilities, travel and overhead, so these numbers are not a profitability claim.

Advance50% of monthly base, one approval/month, no interest, rejected/withdrawn requests
do not consume the allowance. Explicit installment agreements prevent a weekly pay period
being silently emptied by an entire monthly advance.
