# Service photographs

`credits.json` records author, source, license and conversion. Photos retain their source
licenses; CC BY/CC BY-SA require attribution when redistributed. Local files are resized
to at most1100px wide and converted to WebP. CSS may crop their display.
They illustrate services, not PawWorld's actual personnel or facilities.

Each of the 13 demo service codes has a distinct image in `service-visuals.js`.
Walking and home care retain the supplied Figma photographs; the remaining eleven
use the WebP files in this directory. Cards and detail pages share the same mapping.
Pinterest search currently requires login in the automated session; the additional
care photographs were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons with attribution below.
